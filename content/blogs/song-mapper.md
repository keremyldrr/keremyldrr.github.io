A small tool for mapping out the structure of a song while listening to it. Tap along on every bar, mark where the sections change and where the fills are, and it builds a map of the song with the bar count of each section.

<!--more-->

**[Open the Song Mapper](/song_structure/)**

## What it does

- Counts bars as you tap `Space` (or the Bar pad on a phone), and works out the tempo from your taps. You can also tap on every beat instead.
- `Enter` marks a section change, `F` marks a fill, `Backspace` undoes the last tap.
- Name each section and add notes. It writes out the form of the song, like "8 intro, 16 verse, 8 chorus."
- Paste a YouTube link to play the song in the page, with a one-bar count-in at the song's tempo.
- Save songs in your browser, copy the map as text, or save it as a PDF.
